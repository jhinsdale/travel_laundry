Travel Laundry Planner

Plan one clean change for every day, including departure and return.

By John K. Hinsdale of Princeton Junction, NJ USA

Enter departure and return dates.

How this planner works

  • Departure and return dates both count as travel days.
  • You leave wearing C1. Every other change starts clean and packed.
  • A laundry event happens after that day's change is put on. The change being worn is not washed.
  • With next-day service, clothes sent today return after tomorrow's change is chosen. They can be worn the following day. The large L marks the day clothes are sent out.
  • Every schedule gives you a clean change each day. Gray calendar dates are outside the trip. White dates are travel days.
  • A large L marks each laundry day.
  • Only useful options appear. A schedule is omitted when extra laundry would not reduce the number of changes packed.
  • The final option packs one change. More laundry cannot reduce packing further while C1 is being worn.
